We measured salinity, dissolved oxygen, and pH using a YSI meter in all eight tanks on 14 dates within June 4-19, 2025 (112 tank-date observations; no readings were available for June 4 or June 7). Across the recorded observations, mean +/- SD was 36.44 +/- 0.04 for salinity (unitless), 7.06 +/- 0.46 mg/L for dissolved oxygen, and 8.05 +/- 0.04 for pH. Standard deviations describe variation across tanks and sampled dates.
